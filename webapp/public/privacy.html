Privacy

Last updated November 2026. This is a small, one-person project — this page says plainly what is actually collected and why, not a template.

If you never sign in

Your module progress (not started / in progress / done) is stored only in your browser (IndexedDB) and never sent anywhere. Every page you open records one log line server-side — the path, the time, and the referring page — tagged anonymous. That log is used only to build the visitor-count dashboard the site owner can see; it isn't shared, sold, or used for advertising, and there is no advertising or third-party analytics/tracking script on this site at all.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in uses Google's standard OAuth consent screen — this site never sees or stores your Google password. What it does receive and keep, tied to your Google account, is:

  • Your email address, name, and profile picture (from Google, shown back to you in the app)
  • Your module progress, private notes, bookmarks, and a day-streak counter — all visible only to you
  • Reactions (👍/🔥/🤔) you leave on a page — other visitors only ever see the total count, never who reacted
  • Comments you post — these are public: your name and comment text are visible to every visitor, the same as any comment section
  • Articles you write in the writing studio. A draft is visible only to you. A published article is public: its text, pictures, topics and date, and the author name you chose, are visible to every visitor and, once the site owner has approved it (or trusts you as an author), to search engines. Readers see only how many people liked an article, never who
  • Your author name and page. When you first publish you choose a display name and a handle (the address of your page, /authors/your-handle), and may add a short bio and a website, and choose to show your Google profile picture (off by default). Your e-mail address and Google account are never shown
  • Saved articles and reports. What you save is private. A report about an article goes only to the admin
  • Posts, book recommendations, ideas and questions you add in the community area, under your name, and the votes you cast on them: public, like comments
  • Your workspace: pages, folders, study plans (with your target date), revision lists (which lessons you revise, and the day you last revised each) and saved diagrams: private, visible only to you
  • Quiz tries and design work. Your quiz answers and scores, and the exercises and design challenges you write up (text, diagrams, code), are private to you and to the site's admin, who may read a submission to review it. The review is shown to you. A solution is public only if you choose to share it, and is then shown with your author name; you can take it back at any time
  • Messages you send the site owner ("Message Tapan" in the account menu) — these are private, visible only to the admin, and include your name/email so a reply is possible
  • The pageview log above, except when signed in it's tagged with your email instead of anonymous — visible only to the site's one admin, for the same visitor-count purpose

This is held in a single signed-in session cookie (30 days) and in a Cloudflare D1 database; files you upload are stored in Azure Blob Storage (East US region). Nothing here is sold or shared with anyone outside this project, and there's no payment or billing data anywhere because nothing on this site costs money.

Pictures you add to an article, a post, a design submission or a workspace page are stored in Azure Blob Storage and have a public address: anyone who has the link can open the picture. They are not removed when you delete your account (they are no longer linked to you, and the file names are random); write to the address below and I will remove one.

The writing assistant

When the site has it switched on, the writing studio shows an Assistant button. Only when you press a button there, the text you selected, or the article's text, is sent to an AI service (Cloudflare Workers AI or Anthropic, depending on how the site is set up) to get a suggestion back. Nothing is sent otherwise, nothing it suggests is put in your article until you press the button under the suggestion, and this site does not keep what was sent or what came back. The service handles it under its own terms.

Your private workspace

Your workspace is private. Your pages, folders, study plans, revision lists and saved diagrams are visible only to you: they are not on your author page, not in search, and not shown to other readers or to the people who moderate the site's articles. They are kept in the site's database until you delete them or delete your account, which removes all of it at once. Nothing from your workspace is ever published unless you choose to copy a page's text into a new article draft in the writing studio. That makes a separate draft, which only you can see until you publish it; the page itself stays private and unchanged. The site owner can technically read the database, as with every part of the account.

Deleting your data

Sign in, open the account menu (top right), and choose Delete account & data. That immediately and permanently deletes your progress, notes, bookmarks, streak, reactions, workspace pages, votes, quiz tries, design submissions and the reviews of them, the record of your e-mail address, name and picture, and the articles you wrote (drafts and published), your author page, and your likes and saves. Comments you posted keep their place in the thread but lose your name, and their text is replaced with [deleted], so a reply thread someone else is part of doesn't break. Posts, book recommendations and ideas stay, under “[deleted user]”. Messages you sent the owner and the page-view log are anonymized. Pictures you uploaded are not removed (see above). You can also just email the address below and ask.
