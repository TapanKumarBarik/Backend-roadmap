How the tools are checked

Each tool’s arithmetic is a small piece of code with its own tests, kept apart from the page that draws it. The page load calculator names the standards its numbers come from (the handshakes of RFC 8446 and RFC 9000, the initial window of RFC 6928) and says what its model leaves out, so you know where the tool stops and a measurement of your own should take over. The subnet calculator writes out the working for every answer.
