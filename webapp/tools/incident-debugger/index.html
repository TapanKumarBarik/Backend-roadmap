On-Call Engineering Mental Models

1. Mitigate First, Root-Cause Later

During an active P0 incident, your primary duty is to stop the bleeding and restore customer SLOs, not to complete a full forensic investigation. Shedding load, flipping emergency disable flags, or killing stuck queries buys time to fix root cause bugs safely.

2. Beware the "Restart Everything" Trap

Restarting pods or databases during connection pool starvation or retry storms often turns a degraded system into a complete black hole. Hundreds of booting instances immediately hammer downstream dependencies simultaneously during cold start, prolonging recovery.

3. Connection Pool Starvation & Timeouts

A single unindexed query or external HTTP call holding an open database transaction (idle in transaction) blocks connection slots in pools (like HikariCP or PgBouncer). Always configure strict connection acquisition timeouts and database statement timeouts.

4. Blameless Postmortems

Every major production incident reveals a systemic architectural gap: missing timeouts, unbounded memory queues, or lack of circuit breakers. A healthy engineering culture treats incidents as opportunities to automate safeguards and build resilient backends.
