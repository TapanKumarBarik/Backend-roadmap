How Raft Guarantees Safety and Liveness

Leader Election

A node starts as a Follower. If it hears no heartbeats within a randomized timeout (150–300 ms), it increments its Term, becomes a Candidate, and asks peers for votes. Winning a majority ($\lfloor N/2 \rfloor + 1$) transitions it to Leader.

Randomized Election Timeouts

Split votes occur when two nodes become candidates simultaneously. To prevent persistent split-vote ties, Raft randomizes election timeouts so one candidate almost always times out first, collects votes, and wins before others compete.

Quorum Commitment

A log entry is only committed once safely written to a majority of nodes. Once committed, the Raft Leader Completeness property guarantees that every future elected leader will already contain this entry in its log.

Heartbeats & Authority

The leader sends periodic empty AppendEntries heartbeats to prevent followers from timing out. If network partitions isolate the leader, a minority partition cannot commit writes because it cannot achieve a quorum of 3.
